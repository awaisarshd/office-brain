# Joining the team
Optional prompts to use when helpful. Turn a prompt into a task only when you decide to act on it.
## Outcome
Understand the domain, run locally, deliver a small change, and follow the team's delivery process.

## Before joining
- Ask for the first-day plan, contacts, and access checklist.
- Confirm actual sprint dates and how planning and retros work.

## First week
- Collect tracker, repository, documentation, and dashboard links.
- Find contacts for domain, development, testing, and deployment questions.
- Run locally and record a verified testing guide.
- Trace an important business flow and document its rules.
- Learn the definition of done and pull request process.

## First sprint
- Walk through deployment and rollback with a teammate.
- Deliver a small change with the team's reviews and tests.
- Turn answered onboarding questions into knowledge notes.
- Capture an observation for the first retro.
